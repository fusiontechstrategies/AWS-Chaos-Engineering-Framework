# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and technically honest.
- Give and accept useful feedback with empathy.
- Focus disagreement on ideas and evidence, not people.
- Protect private information and security-sensitive details.
- Respect project scope, safety controls, and maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- sexualized language or unwanted attention
- trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate misinformation
- publishing another person's private information without permission
- encouraging unauthorized access or unsafe testing
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers using the contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate confidentiality. Retaliation against a reporter is not permitted.
